# Changelog

## 1.0.20

- Replace the settings status card and standalone action rows with a compact native grouped form and integrated app/version/update row.
- Use the system Settings scene and standard Settings shortcut, with content-based sizing and display-bound position recovery.
- Preserve login approval guidance and the existing five-switch menu-bar panel.

### 简体中文

- 设置去掉运行状态卡片和孤立按钮，改为紧凑的原生分组表单，底部集中显示应用、版本和更新操作。
- 使用系统设置场景与标准快捷键，窗口按内容确定高度，并在显示器变化后回到屏幕内。
- 保留开机自启审批提示和原有五开关菜单栏浮层。
